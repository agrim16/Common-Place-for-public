# Common-Place-for-public
Commonplace: The Ultimate Student Study Guide
Commonplace is an all-in-one, interactive productivity dashboard designed specifically for students. It fuses aesthetic utility with gamified motivation, combining retro design elements with modern AI-driven study tools to keep you focused, organized, and ahead of your curriculum.
🚀 Key Features
• 📅 Dynamic Timetable
	• Tracks your daily class schedule in real-time.
	• Features active visual markers highlighting your current and upcoming periods.
• ⏱️ Focus Timer & Retro Flip Clock
	• Custom Pomodoro Dial Face: Pre-calibrated presets tailored for intense study routines (25-min focus, 15-min revision, 5-min short break, 15-min long break).
	• Audio Alerts: A clean, dual-tone chime sounds automatically at the end of each session.
	• Split-Flap Clock: A beautiful, retro train station-style mechanical digital clock ticking in real-time.
• 🔔 Smart Reminders
	• A consolidated task queue for assignments, projects, and exams.
	• Auditory bell notifications ring immediately when a critical deadline is due.
• 🏆 Marks & XP Gamification
	• Rank Progression: Earn experience points to level up your academic status, climbing the ranks from Freshman all the way to Dean of Studies.
	• Scoreboard & Showcase: Track weekly performance metrics and display earned achievements in a digital trophy case.
• 🤖 Context-Aware AI Study Helper
	• An embedded AI tutor deeply integrated with your personal workspace.
	• Provides contextually grounded assistance by referencing your active timetable, current course progress, and uploaded study notes.
